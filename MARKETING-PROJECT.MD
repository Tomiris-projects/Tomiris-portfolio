# How Marketing Influences Our Purchases

Author: Tomiris Sagenova

## Project Goal
To investigate how marketing influences teenagers' purchasing decisions.

## Research Question
Which marketing tools have the strongest influence on teenagers' purchases?

## Methods
- Literature review
- Survey
- Data analysis
- Presentation

## Results
The study showed that discounts and social media advertising have the strongest impact on purchasing decisions.
